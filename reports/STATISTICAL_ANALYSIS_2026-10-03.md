# Paired Statistical Analysis — 3 October 2026

Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests used matched patient-level values. Holm correction was applied within each horizon × metric family; rank-biserial effect sizes and mean/median paired deltas are retained in the local research package.

- Total comparisons: **1,575** (1,375 architecture-pair comparisons; 200 feature-set comparisons).
- Architecture-pair comparisons with Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 across all metrics: **61**.
- Architecture MAE comparisons with Holm-adjusted p < 0.05: **11**; most short-horizon differences involve persistence versus neural models.
- Feature-set comparisons with Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 across all metrics: **7**.
- Feature-set MAE comparisons with Holm-adjusted p < 0.05: **0**.

These are exploratory single-seed, five-epoch results from 12 patients. Statistical significance does not establish clinical benefit, novelty, or external validity. Review the per-patient table and all effect sizes before writing conclusions.
